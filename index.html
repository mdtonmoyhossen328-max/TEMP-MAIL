FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know about disposable email.

A disposable address is a short-lived inbox used for signups, verification codes and downloads. It keeps your real email private, and you can burn it afterwards so spam never reaches your personal inbox.

Until you delete it, the address is saved in this browser and the inbox keeps working. If an account stays inactive for a long time, the Mail.tm service may purge it automatically — finish important tasks promptly.

When you open a message, its subject and body are scanned for 4–8 digit verification codes. Detected codes appear in a highlighted banner above the message with a one-click copy button.

Yes — MailVault is 100% free with no login, signup or payment. It uses the free public Mail.tm API and collects no personal data. Share it freely with your community.

Never use it for banking, government services, shopping or any important account — once the address is gone, password recovery and critical notifications are gone too. Use it only for temporary, low-stakes tasks.
